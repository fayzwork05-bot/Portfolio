# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4 + GSAP/ScrollTrigger, deployable on Vercel. Chosen by the assistant; the user delegated stack choice.

## Users

Brands, agencies and creators looking to hire a freelance video editor and motion designer, plus recruiters who skim a portfolio in under a minute. (Inferred from the brief, not interviewed.)

## Product Purpose

A single-page portfolio for Fayz Mehmood, a professional video editor and motion designer. Success: a visitor sees the work, understands the services, and contacts Fayz.

## Positioning

The design language is modelled on a Behance reference portfolio (credited to another creator): grid paper, black marquee bands, torn-paper edges, taped kraft notes, glitch-sliced condensed lettering. Reproduce the language, never that creator's portrait, illustration, name, copy or client work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Name: Fayz Mehmood. Everything else about him is UNKNOWN and currently placeholder content.
- Video formats to support: 16:9 (commercial, YouTube) and 9:16 (reels), no stretching, no layout shift.
- Design is expected to change later; keep content in `src/data/` and tokens in CSS variables.
- No deployment, git commits, or publishing of client assets without user approval.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Fayz Mehmood". Behance reference is binding for visual language only.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No client photo, videos, bio, services, tools, contact details, testimonials or achievements exist. All of these are labelled sample content in the UI and in `src/data/`; do not present them as real.

## Product Principles

1. Work leads: the showreel and project videos are the product.
2. Placeholder content is honest: marked as sample, never fabricated as fact.
3. Content and design are separable so both can be swapped.
4. Motion is authored once per moment and always respects reduced motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard focus visible, skip link, prefers-reduced-motion honoured, WCAG AA contrast on text.
